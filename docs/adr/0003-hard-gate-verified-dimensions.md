# Hard gate: no fabrication without verified dimensions

The system must prevent cut lists and purchase orders for long-lead items (glass, stone) from being generated until the OS has official dimensions verified. This is enforced as a status gate in the OS lifecycle: an OS in "Pending Measurement" status cannot transition to "Ready for Production" (which unlocks fabrication and PO generation for glass/stone) until Sales enters verified dimensions. Fabrication with unofficial/customer-provided dimensions is the #1 cause of material waste in fenestration — glass and stone cut to wrong size cannot be resized, and aluminum profiles cut too short become scrap. Aluminum POs can be placed before measurement (since aluminum is cut from stock bars and can often be re-cut), but glass and stone POs must require verified dimensions.
